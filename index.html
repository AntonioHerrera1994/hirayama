Choose Based on Purpose

Two Control Methods

Mode 1

Unsaturated Control

Prevents dew condensation and can set humidity at any value between 65%RH and 100%RH. Supports fixed-value operation and gradient temperature rise/fall. Conforms to IEC 60068-2-66 and JIS C 60068-2-66.

Mode 2

Saturated Control

Humidity is fixed at 100%RH from the rising start time — a wet saturation test where dew condensation occurs on specimens, corresponding to conventional saturated-type pressure cooker testing.

Choose Based on Purpose

Three Exhaust Modes

01

Slow Cooling at Set Humidity

Cools slowly with humidity maintained until temperature reaches 100°C and pressure drops to 0MPa — protects specimens from pressure stress and drying.

02

Cooling With Moisture Maintained

Cools the chamber faster than slow cooling while still minimizing pressure stress and drying.

03

Quick Exhaust

Opens the exhaust valve immediately for the shortest specimen removal time — ideal for specimens unaffected by pressure stress.
